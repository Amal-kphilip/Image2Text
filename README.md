# Img2Text: Social Media Edition

Transform your photos into stunning text art optimized for social media! 🎨✨

**Img2Text** is a powerful Progressive Web App (PWA) that converts images into ASCII, Braille, and Block art directly in your browser. All processing is **100% client-side**, identifying privacy and speed. Your images never leave your device.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🎨 Multiple Art Styles**:
  - **Braille (⣿⣶⣻)**: High-resolution detail using Unicode Braille patterns.
  - **Block (░▒▓█)**: Smooth gradients using shading characters.
  - **Classic ASCII (.:-=+*#%@)**: Retrogaming style text art.
  
- **📱 Social Media Ready**:
  - Generated art is optimized for sharing on WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, X (Twitter), and Reddit.
  
- **✏️ Built-in Image Editor**:
  - Crop and rotate your images before conversion to get the perfect frame.
  
- **🛠️ Advanced Controls**:
  - **Width Slider**: Adjust text resolution (10–100 columns).
  - **Contrast**: Enhance details in darker or lighter images.
  - **Invert**: White-on-black or black-on-white modes.
  
- **🚀 Installable App (PWA)**:
  - Works offline and installs like a native app on iOS, Android, and Desktop.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Upload an Image**: Click the upload button to select a photo from your device.
2. **Edit (Optional)**: Use the built-in editor to crop or rotate your image for better composition.
3. **Choose a Style**: Select your preferred mode: **Braille**, **Block**, or **ASCII**.
4. **Fine-Tune**:
   - Adjust the **Width** slider to change the detail level.
   - Tweak **Contrast** if the output is too dark or washed out.
   - Toggle **Invert** to swap background and foreground colors.
5. **Share**: Click **Copy to Clipboard** and paste your creation into your favorite chat or social media app!

## 🔐 Privacy Focused

No servers. No uploads. Everything runs locally in your browser using modern web technologies (React + Vite + WASM).
